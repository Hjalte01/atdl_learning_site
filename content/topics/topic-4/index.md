## Learning path

Start with [LabelBench: how to choose labels without repeatedly training the full model](/papers/labelbench). Its guide separates selection, final training and inference, then compares label budgets, compute and pool accuracy. Eight recall cards are available in the guide and in [printable study material](/study/print).

Continue with [CEC: calibrate uncertainty, use neighbors and query weighted-cluster representatives](/papers/cec). Its guide traces CLIP probabilities into acquisition and context-only learning, with eight diagrams and eight recall cards.

The live vault supplies six Topic 4 paper PDFs in order: LabelBench, CEC, Active Prompt, nnActive, SUGFW+, and CSCS. LabelBench and CEC have been explained here; the remaining names are filename labels pending source review. The topic’s descriptive grouping above is provisional, rather than an asserted official course title.

Use the [Topic 4 reading library](/materials?topic=4) to read the PDFs and check guide availability.
